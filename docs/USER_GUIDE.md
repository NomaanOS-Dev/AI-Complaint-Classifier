# NomaanOS User Guide

Welcome to **NomaanOS** — a customer complaint management platform designed to help businesses collect customer feedback, understand what matters most, and respond faster with confidence.

---

## 1. Introduction to NomaanOS

### What It Does
NomaanOS gives businesses one central place to collect customer complaints, automatically organize them using AI, track their progress, and review team performance.

### Why You Might Need It
When customer complaints arrive across scattered inboxes or forms, urgent issues can easily be overlooked. NomaanOS keeps every complaint organized in one queue and highlights what needs attention first.

### Where to Find It
Open the NomaanOS website in your browser. From the home page, you can **Sign in** to an existing account, **Create account** to get started, or open **Docs** to read this guide.

### What Happens Afterward
Once you sign up and verify your email, you have your own private workspace where you can record complaints, collect customer submissions through a public link or embedded form, and view AI-generated insights.

---

## 2. Understanding the Dashboard

### What It Does
The **Dashboard** is your home screen after signing in. It gives you quick access to every area of NomaanOS and shows all workspaces you belong to.

### Why You Might Need It
Use the Dashboard whenever you want to switch between workspaces, create a new workspace, or jump straight to your complaints, analytics, or form settings.

### Where to Find It
NomaanOS opens the **Dashboard** automatically after you sign in. You can also return to it anytime by clicking **Dashboard** in the top navigation bar.

### How to Use It
1. Review **Your workspaces** on the left to see each workspace name, your role, and a quick **Analytics** link.
2. Use the top navigation bar to open **Complaints**, **Analytics**, **Integrations**, **Docs**, or **Log out**.
3. Use **Create a workspace** on the right whenever you want to add another workspace.

### What Happens Afterward
Selecting any link in the top navigation bar or workspace list opens that section immediately.

## 3. Creating an Account and Signing In

### What It Does
Registration creates your personal NomaanOS account along with your first workspace. Signing in opens your existing account and workspaces.

### Why You Might Need It
Every team member needs an account to view and manage complaints securely.

### Where to Find It
Click **Create account** or **Sign in** in the top navigation bar on the home page.

### How to Create an Account
1. Open the **Create account** page.
2. Enter your **Full name**, **Email**, a **Password** (12 to 128 characters), and a **Workspace name** (such as your company or team name).
3. Click **Create account**.
4. Alternatively, click **Continue with Google** (when shown) to register using your Google account.

### How to Sign In
1. Open the **Sign in** page.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**—or click **Continue with Google** if you registered with Google.

### What Happens Afterward
- If you registered with email and password, NomaanOS sends a verification email and opens the **Verify Email** page so you can confirm your email address.
- If you signed in with Google or an already-verified account, NomaanOS takes you straight to your **Dashboard**.

---

## 4. Email Verification

### What It Does
Email verification confirms that you own the email address on your account before allowing access to your workspaces and customer complaints.

### Why You Might Need It
Verification protects your account and ensures important account messages—such as password resets—reach the right inbox.

### Where to Find It
You are taken to the **Verify Email** page automatically after registering or signing in with an unverified account.

### How to Use It
1. Open your email inbox and find the **Verify your email address** message from NomaanOS.
2. Click the **Verify Email** button inside the message.
3. If you do not see the email, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification email** on the Verify Email page (wait at least 60 seconds between requests).
4. If you typed the wrong email during sign-up, use **Need to change your email address?**, type the correct address, and click **Update Email & Resend**. *(Email changes here are only available before your account is verified.)*

### What Happens Afterward
Once verified, NomaanOS confirms your email and sends you to your **Dashboard**.

---

## 5. Resetting a Forgotten Password

### What It Does
Lets you regain access to your account by setting a new password through a time-limited email link.

### Why You Might Need It
If you forget your password or want to update it while signed out, you can reset it safely without losing your workspaces or complaints.

### Where to Find It
On the **Sign in** page, click **Forgot Password?** next to the Password field.

### How to Use It
1. Enter the **Email** address registered to your NomaanOS account and click **Send Reset Link**.
2. Open the **Reset your password** email in your inbox within **30 minutes** and click **Reset Password**.
3. Enter a **New Password** (12 to 128 characters) and type it again in **Confirm Password**.
4. Click **Reset Password**, then click **Back to Login**.

### What Happens Afterward
Your password is updated immediately, older reset links stop working, and you are signed out of any other active sessions. You can sign in right away with your new password.

> **Note for Google users:** If you originally created your account using **Continue with Google**, you do not have a separate NomaanOS password—use **Continue with Google** on the Sign in page instead.

## 6. Understanding Workspaces

### What It Does
A **workspace** is a dedicated space that keeps a business or department's complaints, AI insights, analytics, and public contact forms separate from everything else.

### Why You Might Need It
If you manage multiple brands, stores, or support teams, separate workspaces keep each team's complaints and reports organized and isolated.

### Where to Find It
View all of your workspaces on the **Dashboard**, or switch between them using the **Workspace** dropdown at the top of **Complaints**, **Analytics**, and **Integrations**.

### How to Create a Workspace
1. Open the **Dashboard**.
2. In the **Create a workspace** panel, enter a **Workspace name**.
3. Click **Create workspace**.

### What Happens Afterward
The new workspace appears immediately under **Your workspaces** with you listed as its **owner**.

> **Roles and availability:** Workspace **owners** and **admins** can update complaint statuses and manage public or embedded forms. Standard **members** can view complaints, add new complaints, run AI analysis, and view analytics. Inviting additional users into an existing workspace is not currently available in the website interface.

## 7. Creating and Viewing Complaints

### What It Does
Lets your team record customer complaints manually, search and filter existing complaints, and review the full details and history of each issue.

### Why You Might Need It
Whether a customer calls on the phone, visits in person, or submits a form online, keeping every complaint in one list ensures nothing gets lost.

### Where to Find It
Click **Complaints** in the top navigation bar.

### How to Create a Complaint
1. Check that the right workspace is selected under **Active workspace**.
2. In the **Record a complaint** panel, enter a **Title** (required), a **Description** (required), and an optional **Customer email**.
3. Click **Create complaint**.

### How to View, Search, and Filter Complaints
1. Scroll through **Complaint records** (up to 20 per page, using **Previous** and **Next** to browse).
2. Type keywords in **Search complaints** and click **Search** to match words in the title or description.
3. Use the **Filter by status** dropdown to view **All statuses**, **open**, **in progress**, **resolved**, or **closed**.
4. Click any complaint title to open its full detail page, where you can read the entire message, customer email, submission source, and the chronological **Activity timeline** (click **Load more activity** to load older entries).

### What Happens Afterward
Every new complaint starts with status **open** and is automatically queued for AI classification. Once saved, a complaint's title, description, and activity history cannot be edited or deleted.

## 8. Understanding AI Classification Results

### What It Does
As soon as a complaint is created, NomaanOS automatically reads it and assigns a category, urgency assessment, short explanation, and reply draft.

### Why You Might Need It
Instead of reading every incoming message from scratch to figure out which department should handle it, your team gets instant organization and context.

### Where to Find It
You can see a summary of each complaint's classification directly in the **Complaints** list, and full details on the complaint's detail page under **AI Intelligence**.

### How to Use It
1. When a complaint is newly created, it briefly shows **Analysis queued** and then **Analyzing…** before updating automatically with **Analysis Ready**.
2. Open any complaint and click **Analyse complaint** (or **Re-analyse**) whenever you want to generate or refresh its analysis.

### Categories Assigned by AI
| Category | Covers Issues Related To |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Payment** | Failed charges, double billing, declined cards, checkout issues |
| **Refund** | Refund requests, delayed reimbursements, credit disputes |
| **Delivery** | Late shipments, missing packages, tracking problems, wrong items |
| **Account** | Login trouble, locked accounts, subscription or access issues |
| **Technical** | App crashes, bugs, broken pages, service interruptions |
| **Product quality** | Damaged goods, defective products, items below expectations |
| **Other** | General feedback or topics outside the categories above |

### What Happens Afterward
The saved classification appears on the complaint detail page with a short **Reasoning Summary** explaining why that result was chosen, and updates your workspace **Analytics** charts. If the AI service was temporarily unavailable and a basic automated assessment was used instead, a notice is shown so you can click **Re-analyse** later.

## 9. Understanding Sentiment, Severity, and Priority

### What It Does
Evaluates how upset the customer sounds, how serious the problem is, and how urgently your team should respond.

### Why You Might Need It
Not every complaint is equally urgent. Priority scoring helps your team handle critical, high-impact complaints before routine questions.

### Where to Find It
Open any analyzed complaint and look under **AI Intelligence** (and look for the **Critical Issue** banner at the top of urgent complaints).

### How to Read the Results
- **Sentiment:** Shows the customer's tone — **positive**, **neutral**, or **negative**.
- **Severity:** Shows the impact on the customer — **low**, **medium**, **high**, or **critical**.
- **Priority Score and Label:** Rates urgency from **0 to 100** and groups it into four levels:
  - **Low (0–24):** Handle during normal queue review.
  - **Medium (25–49):** Address in standard support rotation.
  - **High (50–79):** Prioritize ahead of routine issues.
  - **Urgent (80–100):** Needs immediate attention.

### What Happens Afterward
Complaints with high urgency display a red **Critical Issue** alert banner at the top of the complaint detail page so anyone opening the ticket sees its urgency immediately.

## 10. Using Suggested Replies

### What It Does
Creates a polite, ready-to-edit **Suggested reply draft** tailored to the customer's complaint.

### Why You Might Need It
Drafting responses from scratch takes time. Starting with a clear, empathetic draft helps support agents reply faster while keeping tone consistent.

### Where to Find It
Open any analyzed complaint and scroll to **Suggested reply draft** at the bottom of the **AI Intelligence** section.

### How to Use It
1. Read the suggested reply and check that all details match your company's policy.
2. Edit the text directly inside the box if you want to personalize the wording.
3. Click **Copy draft** to copy the reply to your clipboard.
4. Paste and send the reply to the customer through your email or support channel.

### What Happens Afterward
Your edited text stays ready to copy. NomaanOS **never** sends replies to customers automatically—a team member always reviews and sends the message.

## 11. Updating Complaint Statuses

### What It Does
Tracks where each complaint stands in your support process, from arrival to resolution.

### Why You Might Need It
Clear statuses keep your team aligned on which complaints still need work and which ones are finished.

### Where to Find It
Workspace **owners** and **admins** can change status from the dropdown on any row in **Complaints**, or from the **Status** dropdown at the top-right of the complaint detail page.

### How to Use It
1. Choose one of the four statuses:
   - **Open:** Newly received and waiting to be handled.
   - **In progress:** Currently being investigated or worked on.
   - **Resolved:** Issue has been solved or answered.
   - **Closed:** Completed and archived.
2. Select the new status from the dropdown.

### What Happens Afterward
The status updates immediately, **Analytics** metrics recalculate automatically, and an entry is added to the complaint's **Activity timeline** recording the change.

## 12. Understanding Analytics

### What It Does
Summarizes complaint volume, open backlog, resolution speed, and AI classification breakdowns for any date range you choose.

### Why You Might Need It
Helps business owners and support leads spot recurring problems, track team efficiency, and see whether complaint volume is rising or falling.

### Where to Find It
Click **Analytics** in the top navigation bar (or click **Analytics** next to a workspace on the Dashboard).

### How to Use It
1. Select the **Workspace** you want to review.
2. Choose **From** and **Through** dates (up to 366 days; defaults to the last 30 days).
3. Click **Apply dates**.

### What You Will See
- **Total complaints:** All complaints created during the selected dates.
- **Unresolved:** Total complaints currently **open** or **in progress**.
- **Resolution rate:** Percentage of complaints in the range that are **resolved** or **closed**.
- **Average resolution:** Average hours taken from complaint creation until resolution.
- **Charts:** Daily **Complaint trend** line chart plus breakdown bars for **Status**, **Categories**, **Priority**, and **Severity**.

### What Happens Afterward
Charts and totals update right away for the selected dates. Reports are viewed interactively on screen; downloading or exporting files is not currently available.

## 13. Setting Up a Public Complaint Form

### What It Does
Creates a dedicated, NomaanOS-hosted web link that customers can open to submit complaints directly into your workspace—no NomaanOS account required.

### Why You Might Need It
Gives you an instant complaint submission page you can link from a **"Submit a Complaint"** button on your website, help center, or customer emails.

### Where to Find It
Click **Integrations** in the top navigation bar (available to workspace **owners** and **admins**) and find the **Public contact form** panel.

### How to Use It
1. Click **Create contact form link**.
2. Copy the generated form link right away and save it securely. *(For privacy, the full link is shown only when created or rotated.)*
3. Optional: Click the **`i`** help icon next to **Public contact form** to view quick instructions and copy ready-made HTML button code for your website.
4. Use **Disable form** / **Enable form** anytime to pause or resume submissions, or click **Rotate form link** to replace the link and cancel the old one.

### What Happens Afterward
When a customer opens your link, fills out the form, and clicks **Send message**, they receive a confirmation message with a **Reference** number, and their complaint appears automatically in your workspace's **Complaints** list.

## 14. Embedding a Complaint Form into a Website

### What It Does
Lets you place a customizable NomaanOS complaint form directly inside a page on your own website so customers never leave your site.

### Why You Might Need It
Keeps your brand experience seamless while sending every customer submission straight into your NomaanOS complaint queue and AI workflow.

### Where to Find It
Open **Integrations**, find **Embed & Integrate Form**, and click **Open setup wizard**.

### How to Use It
1. Select **Option A · Embed Form**.
2. In **1 · Configure**:
   - Enter a **Form display name** shown at the top of the form.
   - Choose which optional fields to include (**Customer name**, **Email address**, **Category**) alongside the required **Complaint title** and **Complaint description**, and mark which optional fields should be required.
   - Under **Websites allowed to embed this form**, list the exact website addresses (1 to 10 addresses, one per line) where the form will appear.
   - Click **Create embed form**.
3. In **2 · Install & test**:
   - Choose your website format (**HTML + JavaScript**, **React**, or **Next.js**).
   - Click **Copy code** and place the snippet into your website page.
   - Submit a quick test complaint to confirm it appears in **Complaints**.
4. You can return to **Your embed forms** anytime to **Edit settings**, **Rotate URL** (replaces the form link), or **Revoke** a form.

### What Happens Afterward
Customers visiting your website see the embedded complaint form directly on your page. Submissions appear immediately in your workspace's **Complaints** list and are analyzed automatically by AI.

## 15. Simple Frequently Asked Questions

**Does NomaanOS send AI replies to customers automatically?**
No. NomaanOS only drafts a suggested reply. A team member always reviews, edits if needed, copies, and sends it manually.

**Why can't I change a complaint's status or open Integrations?**
Only workspace **owners** and **admins** can update complaint statuses or manage Integrations. Standard **members** have view, complaint creation, and AI analysis access.

**Can I edit or delete a complaint after submitting it?**
No. Once created, a complaint's title, description, and activity history are permanent. You can only update its status or re-run AI analysis.

**Can I invite coworkers into my workspace?**
Not in the current version of NomaanOS. Every new user receives their own workspace when they register.

**Can I export Analytics or Complaints to Excel, CSV, or PDF?**
No. Analytics and complaints are viewed directly inside the NomaanOS website.

**Can I change my name, verified email, or password inside a Profile page?**
There is currently no Profile or Account Settings page while signed in. If you forget your password, use **Forgot Password?** on the **Sign in** page.

**Does NomaanOS send notifications when a new complaint arrives?**
No. There is no notification bell or alert email for new complaints. New items appear directly in **Complaints**, and urgent items display a **Critical Issue** banner when opened.

## 16. Basic Troubleshooting

| What Happened | How to Fix It |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Redirected to Verify Email after signing in** | Your email is not verified yet. Open the verification email in your inbox and click **Verify Email**, or click **Resend verification email** on the Verify Email page. |
| **Did not receive the verification email** | Check your spam or junk folder, wait 60 seconds, and click **Resend verification email**. If you typed the wrong address at sign-up, update it under **Need to change your email address?**. |
| **Password reset link says it expired or was already used** | Reset links expire after **30 minutes** and work only once. Go back to **Sign in**, click **Forgot Password?**, and request a fresh link. |
| **No password reset email arrived** | If you originally registered with **Continue with Google**, you do not have a NomaanOS password—click **Continue with Google** on the **Sign in** page instead. |
| **AI analysis says "Analysis queued" or "Analyzing…"** | Wait a few seconds—the page refreshes automatically as soon as classification finishes. |
| **AI analysis says "Analysis failed"** | Open the complaint and click **Analyse complaint** (or **Re-analyse**) to try again. |
| **Public contact form says "This contact form is unavailable"** | The form link was disabled, rotated, or revoked. Ask a workspace owner or admin to re-enable the form or share the active link. |
| **Embedded form does not appear on your website** | Open **Integrations → Embed & Integrate Form**, edit the form, and make sure your website's exact address is listed under **Websites allowed to embed this form**. |
| **Lost your public form link** | For privacy, links are shown only once when created. Click **Rotate form link** (or **Rotate URL** for embed forms) to generate a new link and cancel the old one. |
